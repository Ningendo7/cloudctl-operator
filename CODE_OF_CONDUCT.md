# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Everyone taking part in this project - asking questions, reporting bugs,
sending changes - is entitled to a respectful, harassment-free experience,
whatever their background, identity or experience level.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Give and take criticism of the work, not the person.
- Keep discussion on topic and technical.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory language or personal attacks.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a problem privately through the repository's
[private advisory form](https://github.com/Ningendo7/cloudctl-operator/security/advisories/new)
(**Security** tab → **Report a vulnerability**), saying it is a conduct report.
If the form is not available, open an issue asking for a private contact
method, without details. Reports are read by the maintainer and kept
confidential.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues and changes that break
this code, and may block people who do not stop. Reports are handled in good
faith; being reported is not a finding.
